STELAR-Pro 12-taxon / 2-gene-tree duplicate test

Design:

Score-only cross-check for the inferred tree:

  NORMALIZED_TRIPLET_SCORE = 312 / (2 * C(12,3)) = 312 / 440

Independent per-gene-tree score breakdown:

Important scoring note:
  2 * C(12,3) = 440 is the maximum possible total rooted triplet count across
  two fully resolved 12-taxon gene trees. STELAR-Pro's score here is lower
  because ASTRAL-Pro roots/tags the two duplicated gene trees into different
  speciation-rooted bipartition structures. STELAR-Pro then scores only those
  speciation-driven bipartitions, with duplicated taxa collapsed once.

Note:
  STELAR-Pro optimizes triplet score. This file is for triplet-score
  cross-checking, not quartet-score validation.
